ncnn — vendored neural-network inference library (OCR engine)
============================================================

Bundled as   : lib/arm64-v8a/libncnn_jni.so — ncnn is statically linked into the
               app's JNI library (app/src/main/cpp/CMakeLists.txt), together with
               the app's own C++ wrappers (DetNcnn / RecNcnn / KanaSizeNcnn).
Vendored at  : app/src/main/cpp/ncnn/include (headers) and
               app/src/main/cpp/ncnn/lib/arm64-v8a/libncnn.a (Git LFS)

Licence text : licenses/texts/bsd-3-clause-ncnn.txt

               also reproduces the notices for the third-party components ncnn
               itself contains — e.g. the zlib-licensed neon/sse/avx mathfun
               headers — so nothing further is needed for those.)

Upstream     : https://github.com/Tencent/ncnn
Built from   : https://github.com/vgf89/ncnn
Pinned ref   : a2b8507f6a3449e80f1c2585a127e3c08e3a9f3f (fork master, FORK_PIN in
               tools/build_ncnn.sh). Upstream commit 2130e00 is documented as the
               base in docs/ncnn-conversion.md.
Build script : tools/build_ncnn.sh — clones the fork at the pin, enforces the
               required patches by marker grep and enforces the resulting blob
               (libncnn.a must contain the fused-GELU code) before it may ship.

Modifications
-------------

The vendored library is the fork above, not upstream ncnn: three local fixes are
merged into it (int8 1x1 convolution on flattened 1D blobs, ConvolutionDepthWise
int8 scale terms 201/202, and fused GELU activation type 7). The `git format-patch`
mboxes are kept as a record in third_party/ncnn-patches/; they are modifications to
BSD-3-Clause source and are distributed under the same licence. Copyright over the
unmodified parts remains with Tencent and ncnn's other contributors.
