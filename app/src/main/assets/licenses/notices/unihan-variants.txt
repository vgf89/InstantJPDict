The pair direction is taken from the recogniser's own vocabulary (canonical = the
side present in PP-OCRv6_small_ncnn/vocab.json) or, for the JMdict half, from the
oK/rK tag. Per-pair citations are recorded in
app/src/main/assets/variants/PROVENANCE.txt, so each line of the shipped table
names the field it came from.
