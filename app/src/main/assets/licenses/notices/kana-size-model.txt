Why CC BY-SA 4.0
----------------

The weights carry no expression from either corpus — a 47,425-parameter model that predicts the
small/large form of a kana from five characters of context on each side cannot memorise text — so
whether the share-alike condition reaches them at all is genuinely contested. Creative Commons'
own guidance for training ("Using CC-Licensed Works for AI Training", 2025) takes the cautious
line: a model based on share-alike content and shared publicly should carry the same licence.
Adopting the corpus's terms removes the question, costs nothing, and matches how the pitch-accent
and KRADFILE components bundled in this same app are already handled. Attribution for both corpora
is above.

What the model is used for
--------------------------

Deciding the small/large form of っ/つ, ゃ/や, ゅ/ゆ, ょ/よ from five characters of context on each
side, behind the "Correct small/large kana with the size model" checkbox (issue #44). Verified
on-device against ten published logits by KanaSizeNcnn.selfCheck.
