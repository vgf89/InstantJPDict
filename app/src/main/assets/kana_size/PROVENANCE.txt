Verified
--------

The ten published logits in the artifact's validation_vectors.json reproduce to 3.8e-06 under
ncnn (host harness, fp32 — fp16, bf16 and packing pinned off, since fp16 alone costs 1.5e-03).
An independent direct C++ forward pass, since deleted, agreed to 5.8e-06. KanaSizeNcnn.selfCheck
re-runs the same ten vectors through this device's own encoder and JNI.
